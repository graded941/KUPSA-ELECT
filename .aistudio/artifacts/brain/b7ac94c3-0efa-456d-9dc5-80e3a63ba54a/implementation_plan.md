• Extreme Mobile Optimization: Engineered for sub-second page loads on basic smartphones and low-bandwidth 3G data connections, requiring less than 1.5MB of total data to participate.
• Zero Duplicate Voting: Single-use cryptographic nonces prevent double voting, browser refreshing exploitation, or ballot stuffing.
